# ABM website concept and review

A website concept for the Anglican Board of Mission and Anglicans in Development: a homepage, a donate page (`#donate`) and the Season of Creation 2026 appeal (`#season-of-creation`), with a review of the current site in `review/` and a second, forward-looking concept in `future/`. Prepared by Singh Studio in October 2026. None of them is the live site, and all the pages are set to stay out of search engines.

The words, photographs and logos belong to ABM and AID and their partners, and are used only to illustrate the ideas.
